NEW MATCH ENTRY

So you have just finished an adrenaline-fueled Call of Duty multiplayer match? Enter your stats here and add them to the records of triumph...or shame.

The stats currently being recorded include:

  • Player
  • Game
  • Map
  • Game Mode
  • Score
  • Eliminations/Kills
  • Deaths
  • K/D Ratio
  • Win/Loss

Once the stats are entered, press the "New Game" button. A draft copy of the results will be displayed for review. Once you are happy with the record, hit "Save Game" to enter the record into the database.
